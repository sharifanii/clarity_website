About

Mohsen Sharifani, PhD

Founder, Clarity · United States

Mohsen Sharifani, PhD, believes the most valuable thing he can offer a client is clarity — on people, processes, and products.

Over 14 years at Apple, Dell, and Stitch Fix, Mohsen built teams, developed products, and established the processes and partnerships that allowed organizations to operate at scale. He learned that the difference between companies that grow and companies that stall rarely comes down to ideas. It comes down to focus.

Today he brings that organizational discipline to founders and business owners who are ready to grow — helping them cut through the noise, build the right systems, and lead with conviction.

Outside of work, Mohsen is drawn to nature, making things with his hands, and the kind of conversations that change how you see a problem.
